Initialisation 
After plasma burning procedure, the base pressure did not go down to the expected value 
of 2x10-5 mbar. Troubleshooting measures revealed that the plasma chamber is vacuum 
tight with respect to the container atmosphere. However, a leak of the order of 0.08 sccm 
through the nominally closed IQF200 needle valve was discovered. Gas flow to be taken 
into account in preparation of the next campaign. Other troubleshooting/corrective 
measures in discussion.
