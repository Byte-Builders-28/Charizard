Other issues 
At the end of Day 1, the leak rate through the IQF suddenly doubled. Injection of 
microparticles became practically impossible. On days 2 and 3 the experiments were 
performed with the flexhose disconnected from the gas bottle and pumped down to achieve 
the acceptable leak rate. Air (nitrogen) admixture was seen in the plasma emission spectra. 
Reason: unavoidable air inclusion during the flexhose connection. Polluted gas needs to be 
pumped out before the experiment. On science day 3, the fourth run of Microparticle Charge 
and Ion Drag had to be interupted due to the wrong gas in the plasma chamber (no neon
